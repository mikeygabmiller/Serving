# Positions: two servers at Low Mass, and every server at a sung and a solemn High Mass

Researched 2026-10-05 for the position sheets (which show one server's places and
actions, Mass by Mass). This file adds to `serving-the-tlm.md`; read that first for
the general rules, the rubric texts and the source list.

## Sources used here

Keys are those in `data/sources.js`.

- **FSSPOM**: Immaculate Conception, Omaha (FSSP), *Knights of the Altar* role sheets,
  June 2023. Read in full:
  - *Low Mass, Two Servers*;
  - *Sung Mass*: Master of Ceremonies, Thurifer, Acolytes & Crucifer, Torchbearers;
  - *Solemn Mass*: Master of Ceremonies, Thurifer, Acolytes & Crucifer, Torchbearers.

  These are the only current, step-by-step, role-by-role sheets found. They describe
  one parish's sanctuary (a "server sacristy", a pillar, a windowpane, stools on the
  Epistle side), so the site keeps their order of actions and drops their landmarks.
- **FORT**: Fortescue, *The Ceremonies of the Roman Rite Described*, 2nd ed., 1920:
  - ch. X §6, two servers at Low Mass (pp. 82–83);
  - ch. XI, High Mass: §2 procession and cross (pp. 86–87), §3 Asperges (pp. 87–90),
    §4 thurifer (pp. 90–94), §5 acolytes (pp. 94–98), §6 torchbearers (pp. 98–99),
    §7 MC (pp. 99–105);
  - figures 6–13, where everyone stands at High Mass (pp. 126–129);
  - ch. XIII, Sung Mass without deacon and subdeacon, both forms (pp. 136–143).

  He wrote before the 1960 rubrics; the changes that touch servers are listed below.
- **MR62 / CR60**: the 1962 *Ritus servandus* and Code of Rubrics, as quoted and checked
  in `serving-the-tlm.md` (section F).

## What the 1960 rubrics changed for these servers (High)

- **Incense** is allowed at every sung Mass (CR60 n. 426). Fortescue (p. 137) calls it
  forbidden without deacon and subdeacon unless an approved custom allows it.
- **No incensing of the celebrant after the Gospel at a sung Mass**: "postquam autem
  Evangelium cantaverit, celebrans non incensatur" (MR62 Rit. serv. VI.8). Fortescue
  (p. 140) has the MC incense him there. FSSP Omaha's Sung Mass sheets do not, which
  matches 1962. At a Solemn Mass the deacon still incenses him after the Gospel
  (MR62 Rit. serv. VI.5; FSSPOM Solemn Mass Thurifer 2E).
- **The Communion Confiteor** is omitted by the rubric (CR60 n. 503). FSSP Omaha's
  sheets have the servers say it ("Recite 2nd Confiteor"). Local custom: the sheet
  maker makes it an option.
- **Torches**: "duo saltem" (at least two), lit at the end of the Preface; they leave
  after the elevation of the chalice, or after Communion when there are communicants,
  and stay to Communion on fast days and at Requiems (MR62 Rit. serv. VIII.8).
- **Solemn Mass Epistle**: the celebrant no longer reads it himself; he sits and listens
  (MR62 Rit. serv. VI.4; CR60 n. 473).

## Low Mass with two servers

**Sources:** FSSPOM *Low Mass, Two Servers* (cited by its numbered sections); FORT
pp. 82–83.

FSSP Omaha calls the two servers **Acolyte 1 (AC1)** and **Acolyte 2 (AC2)**.
Fortescue calls them the first and second server. The site uses "first server
(Acolyte 1)" and "second server (Acolyte 2)".

**Places (they differ, so the site says so):**

- **FSSP Omaha.** Each server keeps one corner of the lowest step: AC1 at the Epistle
  corner, AC2 at the Gospel corner (§2B, §9A, §17C). In procession they walk shoulder
  to shoulder, AC1 on the right (Procession).
- **Fortescue.** The second server "changes places with the first, kneeling or standing
  always on the other side" (p. 82).

The sheets follow FSSP Omaha because its sheet is step by step; each step's notes give
Fortescue's alternative where it matters.

**Sequence (FSSPOM section in brackets).** Responses are said by both.

1. **Before Mass.** AC1 helps the priest vest. AC1 brings holy water to the priest, then
   to AC2, and rings the sacristy bell (Procession and Ceremony).
2. **Arrival** (§1).
   - AC1 receives the biretta with the *solita oscula*.
   - Both genuflect, and both hold up the alb as the priest goes up to set the chalice.
   - AC1 takes the biretta to the sedilia, not the credence.
   - When the priest is back at the foot, both kneel as he genuflects.
3. **Prayers at the foot** (§2).
   - Both answer.
   - Profound bow right after the priest's Confiteor. Turn to the priest for the
     *Misereatur* and at *tibi, pater* / *te, pater*.
   - Upright again at the *Amen* before *Indulgentiam*. Sign yourself at *Indulgentiam*.
   - Bow the head from *Deus, tu conversus* until the priest goes up.
   - Then rise and go to your corners of the lowest step and kneel together.
4. **Introit, Kyrie, Gloria, Collect** (§3–6).
   - Sign yourselves at the Introit.
   - Alternate the Kyrie.
   - Do not say the Gloria; sign yourselves with the priest.
   - *Amen* after the Collect conclusion(s).
5. **Epistle** (§7).
   - AC2 has the better view of the priest's hand and leads the *Deo gratias*.
   - AC1 rises and goes, round the lowest step, to the Epistle side where the priest
     reads the Gradual.
6. **Missal to the Gospel side** (§8).
   - AC1 carries it, going straight to the centre *in plano* and genuflecting there.
   - AC2 rises when AC1 genuflects with the missal.
   - The missal is set at an angle at the Gospel side.
7. **Gospel** (§9).
   - AC1 waits at the Gospel side on the first step down from the footpace. Both sign
     themselves.
   - AC1 waits for the Holy Name, then leaves, turning to the left, and crosses
     (genuflecting at the centre) back to the Epistle side and stands facing the missal.
   - AC2 stands at the Gospel corner and does not step back to let AC1 pass.
   - *Laus tibi, Christe* when the priest lifts the book to kiss it. Both kneel at once.
8. **Sermon** (§9B).
   - Stand as the priest comes down and genuflect with him at the centre.
   - Sit on stools at the Epistle side, AC1 to the right of AC2.
   - Afterwards genuflect with him and go back to kneel.
9. **Creed** (§10). Sign yourselves at *Et vitam venturi saeculi*.
10. **Offertory** (§11A–B).
    - After *Oremus* both rise, go to the centre and genuflect.
    - AC1 takes both cruets from the credence, goes to the Epistle-side steps and hands
      the water to AC2.
    - AC2 goes straight up the centre, takes the chalice veil from the priest, folds it
      in thirds, lays it toward the Epistle corner, and comes down the side steps to
      AC1's left.
    - Both wait at the Epistle side. They bow when the priest turns, go up to the top
      step, kiss the cruets and present them: AC1 the wine, AC2 the water, each in the
      right hand.
    - Each kisses the cruet when it comes back, bows and leaves.
11. **Lavabo** (§11C).
    - AC1 unfolds the towel; AC2 holds the water cruet (right hand) and the dish
      (left hand).
    - They approach from the Epistle side, AC2 outside the front edge of the altar, and
      bow before and after.
    - Then AC1 takes the bell. They go back to their places, genuflecting together at
      the centre.
12. **Secret, Preface dialogue** (§12): answer.
13. **Sanctus** (§13). AC1 rings three times. Sign yourselves at the end.
14. **Canon** (§14).
    - AC1 rings once at the *Hanc igitur*, when the priest spreads his hands over the
      chalice.
    - After he takes his hands away, both rise (AC1 with the bell), meet in the centre,
      go up to the footpace, one on each side of the priest, and kneel. No genuflection
      before going up.
    - At each elevation AC1 rings 1 (genuflection), 3 (elevation), 1 (genuflection).
      Both raise the chasuble, not too high.
    - Afterwards both rise, come down to the centre, genuflect and go back to their
      places.
15. **Pater noster, Agnus Dei** (§15–16): *Sed libera nos a malo*; strike the breast.
16. **Communion** (§17).
    - AC1 rings three times at the priest's *Domine, non sum dignus*, on the word
      *dignus*.
    - After the priest drinks the Precious Blood, AC1 takes the paten from the credence.
      Both meet the other servers at the centre (AC2 at the Gospel end of the line),
      genuflect, go up to the top step and kneel, bow profoundly and say the "second
      Confiteor". This Confiteor is FSSP Omaha's practice; the 1962 rubric omits it.
    - AC1 receives, passes the paten to AC2, waits for AC2 to receive, then comes down,
      genuflects and kneels at the Epistle end of the lowest step.
    - AC2 receives, then stands at the priest's right and holds the paten for the
      people's Communion.
    - When the priest goes back to the altar, AC2 holds up the alb, genuflects and kneels
      at the Gospel corner.
17. **Ablutions** (§18A).
    - When the tabernacle is locked, both stand, meet at the centre, genuflect and go to
      the credence: AC1 takes the wine, AC2 the water.
    - They wait at the Epistle-side steps. AC1 goes up alone and pours wine for the first
      ablution.
    - AC1 then stands at the Epistle side on the top step, and AC2 joins AC1. They bow
      when the priest turns.
    - AC1 pours wine over the priest's fingers, then AC2 pours water.
    - They bow, leave, and put the cruets back. AC1 picks up the paten.
18. **Missal and chalice veil** (§18B).
    - Both go to the centre and genuflect, then round the lowest step: AC1 to the Gospel
      side for the missal, AC2 to the Epistle side for the chalice veil. AC2 passes
      behind AC1.
    - They meet at the centre and genuflect. AC1 takes the missal to the Epistle side;
      AC2 takes the veil to the Gospel side and helps the priest "build" the chalice
      (burse, then veil, then burse).
    - AC1 waits at the Epistle side until AC2 has finished.
19. **Postcommunion, dismissal, blessing** (§18C): answer, and *Amen* to the blessing.
20. **Last Gospel** (§19A–B).
    - Stand after the blessing and sign yourselves.
    - AC1 faces the Gospel altar card.
    - Both genuflect at *Et Verbum caro factum est*.
21. **Leonine prayers and leaving** (§19C–D, §20).
    - AC1 fetches the prayer card if needed, returns it afterwards, and fetches the
      biretta.
    - All genuflect. AC1 hands the biretta with the *solita oscula*.
    - In the sacristy: bow to the cross. *Prosit* / *Pro omnibus et singulis*, then
      *Iube, domne, benedicere*, and kneel for the blessing.

**Where Fortescue differs** (pp. 82–83):

- First server hands the wine, the second the water; at the Lavabo the first holds the
  towel, the second the cruet and dish. This is the same as FSSP Omaha.
- Both lift the chasuble. Same.
- At the ablutions the first server serves alone; FSSP Omaha has the second pour the
  water.
- After Communion the first carries the missal and the second the chalice veil; they
  genuflect together, the first in front. Same.

## Missa cantata (sung Mass with servers)

**Sources:** FSSPOM *Sung Mass* sheets (MC, Thurifer, Acolytes & Crucifer,
Torchbearers); FORT ch. XIII §2 "the more solemn form" (pp. 137–143) and §1 "the simpler
form" (p. 136).

**The two forms (FORT pp. 136–137):**

- **The simpler form** has one or two acolytes and is served "almost the same as at Low
  Mass". When the celebrant sits, the acolytes go to the seat, the first hands the
  biretta, and they stand on either side facing each other (some sit on a bench).
- **The more solemn form** has an MC, thurifer, acolytes and torchbearers.

The sheets model the more solemn form; for the simpler form use the Low Mass sheets plus
the seat duty.

**Procession** (FSSPOM MC, Procession; FORT p. 137):

- FSSP Omaha, celebrant not in cope: thurifer; then Acolyte 2, crucifer, Acolyte 1;
  then torchbearers; then MC; then celebrant.
- FSSP Omaha, celebrant in cope (Asperges): AC2, crucifer, AC1; then torchbearers; then
  thurifer, celebrant and MC together.
- Fortescue: thurifer, acolytes with candles, the future torchbearers, MC, celebrant.

**Moment by moment** (role, then what that role does; FSSPOM section numbers):

1. **Arrival.**
   - MC takes the biretta to the sedilia, the place nearer the altar (MC 1).
   - Acolytes put their candles by the credence; the crucifer puts the cross in its
     stand; then all go to their seats (Acolytes & Crucifer, Procession).
   - Thurifer: genuflects at the foot and takes the thurible out (Thurifer, Procession).
   - Torchbearers: genuflect two by two and go to their seats (Torchbearers).
2. **Asperges** (Sundays; MC 2; FORT pp. 137–138).
   - MC hands the sprinkler, holds the cope and walks at the celebrant's right.
   - AC1 takes the aspersorium and book away afterwards.
3. **Prayers at the foot.**
   - MC kneels and answers (MC 3; FORT p. 138).
   - Others kneel at their places.
   - Thurifer: goes for the thurible at *Deus, tu conversus* (Thurifer 1A).
4. **Incensing the altar at the Introit** (MC 4; Thurifer 1; Acolytes 2; FORT
   pp. 138–139).
   - MC and thurifer go up at the Epistle side. The MC takes the boat and hands the
     spoon with the oscula, saying *Benedicite, Pater reverende*.
   - The MC hands the thurible to the celebrant.
   - FSSP Omaha: MC and thurifer accompany the celebrant round the altar, and AC1
     removes the missal and puts it back.
   - Fortescue: the MC moves the missal, or, if MC and thurifer accompany the
     celebrant, the first acolyte does.
   - The MC incenses the celebrant from the floor (three double swings), the thurifer on
     the MC's left.
5. **Introit and Kyrie.** MC at the missal, at the celebrant's right; points out the
   Introit; alternates the Kyrie (MC 4C, 5).
6. **Gloria and seats** (MC 6; FORT p. 139).
   - When the celebrant sits, the MC stands in front of the sedilia facing the people.
     The MC never sits.
   - Others sit at their places.
7. **Collect and Epistle** (MC 6–7). MC at the missal. The Epistle is sung by the
   celebrant or by a lector or server (CR60 n. 514; MR62 Rit. serv. VI.8).
8. **Before the Gospel** (MC 8A; Thurifer 2A–C; Acolytes 3A; FORT p. 139).
   - Incense is put in at the Epistle side.
   - The acolytes take their candles. They line up in the middle: the MC in front, then
     AC2, thurifer, AC1. All genuflect at the MC's signal.
   - The MC (FSSP) carries the missal across. Fortescue has the MC move the missal and
     the acolytes and thurifer genuflect behind the MC.
9. **The Gospel** (MC 8B–C; Thurifer 2C–D; Acolytes 3A; FORT pp. 139–140).
   - The acolytes stand at the Gospel side on the floor, in line with the altar, facing
     the Epistle side, behind the missal: AC2 first, then thurifer, then AC1. FSSP
     Omaha's diagram puts AC2 level with the celebrant and AC1 level with the MC.
   - The MC stands at the corner of the footpace by the book, hands the thurible
     (oscula) for the incensing of the book and takes it back.
   - **1962:** the celebrant is not incensed after the Gospel.
   - Afterwards all go to the middle, genuflect, and the acolytes put the candles back.
10. **Sermon, Creed.**
    - MC goes to the place by the sedilia and signals the kneel at the sung *Et
      incarnatus est*.
    - Thurifer lights fresh charcoal during the Creed (MC 8D–9; Thurifer 2D).
11. **Offertory, cruets** (MC 10A; Acolytes 3A).
    - MC folds the chalice veil.
    - The acolytes stand on the floor at the Epistle side, AC1 at the right with the
      wine, AC2 at the left with the water. They bow, go up, and hand the cruets in the
      right hand with the oscula.
12. **Offertory, incensing** (MC 10B–C; Thurifer 3; Acolytes 3B–C; FORT pp. 140–141).
    - MC and thurifer go up; incense is blessed.
    - AC1 goes the long way to the Gospel side to remove the missal, then puts it back
      after the Gospel side has been incensed. Fortescue: the thurifer does it, or the
      first acolyte if MC and thurifer accompany the celebrant.
    - The MC incenses the celebrant, the thurifer on the MC's left. Then the thurifer
      incenses, in order:
      - the MC (one double swing);
      - the acolytes and crucifer together (centre, left, right);
      - the torchbearers;
      - the people, from the entrance of the sanctuary (three double swings: centre,
        left, right).
13. **Lavabo** (Acolytes 3C; FORT p. 141). AC1 has the towel, AC2 the water and dish. They
    wait on the floor until the celebrant has been incensed.
14. **Sanctus and torches** (Thurifer 3; Torchbearers; Acolytes 4A; FORT pp. 141–142).
    - The torchbearers go out with the thurifer at the Preface and come back in pairs at
      the *Sanctus*. They genuflect, fan out and kneel in a line across the sanctuary.
    - AC1 rings three times at the *Sanctus*.
15. **Consecration** (MC 11; Thurifer 4; Acolytes 4; FORT pp. 141–142).
    - Incense is put in without a blessing. FSSP Omaha: the crucifer does it at the
      *Hanc igitur*. Fortescue: the thurifer or the second acolyte, at *Qui pridie*.
    - The thurifer kneels on the first step at the Epistle side, facing the Gospel side,
      and incenses the Host and the chalice with three double swings each.
    - The MC kneels on the footpace at the celebrant's left and raises the chasuble.
    - AC1 rings once at the *Hanc igitur*, then 1-3-1 at each elevation.
    - Torchbearers stay through Communion when there are communicants (FSSP Omaha: always,
      since there always are).
16. **Communion** (MC 14; Acolytes 6; Thurifer 5; Torchbearers).
    - The servers line up at the foot: MC, AC1, AC2, crucifer, thurifer. They genuflect,
      go up, kneel on the footpace and receive.
    - Which end is which: FSSP Omaha's diagrams are drawn as seen from the nave (their
      Gospel diagrams only make sense that way: "AC2 - C" puts the second acolyte nearer
      the Gospel wall than the celebrant). So the MC, who comes from the Gospel side step,
      is at the Gospel end and the thurifer at the Epistle end; AC1 leads the others from
      the credence. The plan draws it so (first version had it mirrored; fixed 2026-10-05).
    - FSSP Omaha says the "2nd Confiteor" here; the 1962 rubric omits it.
    - The MC goes with the celebrant to distribute Communion (FSSP: "with the paten").
    - The two outside torchbearers go to the far ends of the rail and kneel there until
      Communion is over.
17. **Ablutions, missal, chalice veil** (Acolytes 7; MC 15A).
    - AC1 pours the wine. AC2 brings a paten and waits on the top step.
    - At the second ablution AC1 pours a little wine and AC2 most of the water.
    - After genuflecting at the centre, AC1 takes the missal to the Epistle side and AC2
      the chalice veil to the Gospel side, and helps build the chalice. The MC follows
      the missal across to point out the Communion antiphon.
    - The torches go out when the tabernacle is closed (Torchbearers).
18. **Blessing and Last Gospel** (MC 15B–16; Acolytes 8; Thurifer 6; Torchbearers).
    - All kneel for the blessing.
    - After signing themselves at the Last Gospel, the acolytes take their candles and
      the crucifer the cross, and form up in the middle: torchbearers in front, then
      AC2, crucifer, AC1, with the thurifer behind.
    - The crucifer, and the acolytes beside the crucifer, do not genuflect: they bow.
    - The MC brings the biretta.
19. **Recession.** All turn; the thurifer, now in front, leads out.

## Solemn High Mass

**Sources:** FSSPOM *Solemn Mass* sheets (MC, Thurifer, Acolytes & Crucifer,
Torchbearers); FORT ch. XI §§2–7 and figures 6–13. The deacon (D) and subdeacon (SD) are
clergy; the site shows them on the plan but makes no sheet for them.

**Procession** (FSSPOM Solemn MC):

- Not in cope: thurifer; AC2, crucifer, AC1; MC; SD; D; celebrant.
- In cope: thurifer; AC2, crucifer, AC1; MC; then SD, celebrant and D abreast.
- Fortescue (p. 87): thurifer, AC2, crucifer, AC1, torchbearers in pairs, choir, MC,
  SD, D, celebrant.

**Places** (FORT figs 6–13, and §§4–5):

- The acolytes' usual place is in front of the credence, facing across the sanctuary
  (FORT p. 97; figs 8–10, 13).
- The thurifer stands on the Epistle side of the sanctuary (pp. 91–92).
- The MC is at the celebrant's side, or at the deacon's right in front of the sedilia
  when the ministers sit (fig. 8).
- The torchbearers kneel in a line across the middle of the sanctuary (fig. 13).

**Moment by moment:**

1. **Arrival.**
   - MC takes the birettas: celebrant's and deacon's to the sedilia nearer the altar,
     subdeacon's further (MC 1).
   - Acolytes and crucifer as at Sung Mass.
2. **Asperges** (Sundays). The deacon hands the sprinkler. The MC hands the aspersorium
   to the deacon and walks at the celebrant's right (MC 2; FORT pp. 88–90).
3. **Prayers at the foot.**
   - MC kneels and answers with the ministers (MC 3).
   - Fortescue: the MC kneels at the deacon's right, behind him (p. 100).
4. **Incensing at the Introit** (MC 4; Thurifer 1; FORT pp. 100–101, fig. 6).
   - The MC takes the boat from the thurifer and hands it to the deacon.
   - The thurifer hands the thurible to the deacon.
   - The MC removes the missal while the Epistle side is incensed. Fortescue notes some
     churches have the thurifer do this.
   - The deacon incenses the celebrant. The thurifer stands a little behind at his
     right (FORT fig. 6); FSSP Omaha has the thurifer stand back on the floor and not bow.
5. **Introit, Kyrie.** The celebrant, D and SD form a semicircle at the missal; the MC
   is at the celebrant's right (FORT fig. 7; MC 4C).
6. **Gloria and seats** (MC 6; FORT fig. 8).
   - The MC stands at the deacon's right, facing down the church.
   - The acolytes help at the seats (FORT p. 95); FSSP Omaha has them sit at their
     places.
7. **Epistle.**
   - 1962: "subdiaconus ... cantat Epistolam, quam celebrans sedens auscultat. Epistola
     cantata, celebrans redit ad librum" (MR62 Rit. serv. VI.4). The priest sits; then he
     goes back to the book at the Epistle side, where the SD is blessed. The rubric does not
     say where the deacon is meanwhile, so the plan leaves the D off that drawing.
   - The MC hands the book of lessons to the SD, goes with him to the middle, genuflects,
     and stands at his left while the SD sings the Epistle.
   - Then the MC goes with him to the celebrant for the blessing and takes the book back
     (MC 6B–7B; FORT p. 102).
8. **Before the Gospel** (MC 8A; Thurifer 2; Acolytes 2A; FORT p. 102, fig. 11).
   - Incense is put in.
   - Formation in the middle: SD and D nearest the altar, MC and thurifer behind them,
     then AC2 and AC1 (FSSP Omaha). Fortescue's fig. 11 gives this or the acolytes in
     front.
   - All genuflect at the MC's signal.
9. **The Gospel** (MC 8B–C; Thurifer 2C–D; Acolytes 2A; FORT pp. 96, 102, fig. 12).
   - Before it, the SD carries the missal to the Gospel side and the D puts the book of
     the Gospels on the altar; then incense is put in (MR62 Rit. serv. VI.5). During the
     Gospel the celebrant stands at the Epistle side, and (Fortescue) turns toward the
     Gospel when it begins.
   - Procession to the Gospel place, which Fortescue's fig. 12 puts on the floor at the
     Gospel side of the sanctuary.
   - The SD holds the book between the acolytes, AC1 on his right. The D sings, the MC
     at his right turning the pages, the thurifer near.
   - The MC hands the thurible to the D, who incenses the book.
   - After the Gospel the D incenses the celebrant, the thurifer at his right (FORT
     p. 92; FSSPOM Thurifer 2E). Where: FSSP Omaha has the thurifer and the D walk "back
     to the centre at the foot" for it; Fortescue has the celebrant incensed "standing at
     the same place" where he kissed the book, at the Epistle side. The plan follows FSSP
     Omaha and says so in a note.
10. **Sermon, Creed.**
    - The MC brings the burse to the deacon at the sung *Et incarnatus est* (MC 9B;
      FORT p. 103).
11. **Offertory** (MC 10; Acolytes 3; FORT pp. 96, 103–104).
    - The MC puts the humeral veil on the SD at the credence.
    - AC1 sets the cruets on the dish at the Epistle side of the altar, and the SD hands
      them on. Fortescue: no kisses for the cruets at High Mass, since they go to the
      subdeacon.
    - AC2 folds the chalice veil (Fortescue).
    - Incensing: the MC and thurifer assist. The MC removes the missal (from the Gospel
      side) during the incensing.
    - The D incenses the celebrant, the choir and the SD. The thurifer then incenses the
      D, the MC, the acolytes, crucifer and torchbearers, and the people (FSSPOM
      Thurifer 3; FORT p. 93).
    - The acolytes serve the Lavabo: AC1 the towel, AC2 water and dish (Acolytes 3B;
      FORT p. 96).
12. **Sanctus, torches.** As at Sung Mass (Torchbearers; FORT p. 98).
13. **Consecration** (MC 11; Thurifer 4; FORT pp. 93, 104, fig. 13).
    - The MC puts incense in without a blessing.
    - The thurifer kneels on the first step at the Epistle side, facing the Gospel side,
      and incenses at both elevations. Fortescue has either the MC or the thurifer
      incense.
    - The D kneels at the celebrant's right and raises the chasuble (MR62 Rit. serv.
      VIII.8).
    - Torchbearers kneel in a line.
14. **Pater, Pax.**
    - The thurifer or AC1 takes the humeral veil from the SD at *dimitte nobis* (FORT
      pp. 93–94, 97; FSSPOM MC 12). FSSP Omaha's MC sheet says the thurifer; its thurifer
      sheet does not mention it. Fortescue gives it to AC1, or to the thurifer when the
      acolytes hold torches. The site makes it a parish option (default: the thurifer).
    - At *dimitte nobis* the D and SD go up to the Epistle side of the celebrant (FORT
      p. 105), so the plan puts the SD at the D's right.
    - The pax is given to the servers through the MC (FORT p. 105).
15. **Communion** (MC 14; Acolytes 6; Thurifer 5).
    - Servers line up at the foot: thurifer, AC1, AC2, crucifer, MC (FSSP Omaha). They
      kneel at the foot until the D and SD have received, then go up and kneel on the
      footpace.
    - Read as seen from the nave, the thurifer is at the Gospel end and the MC at the
      Epistle end. That fits the text: the thurifer leads the others from the Epistle side
      and they "go behind MC to get to your spot". No servers' Confiteor on these sheets.
    - Fortescue: after the pax the deacon goes to the celebrant's left and stays there till
      after the ablutions (ch. XI §9, the deacon), so the plan shows the D at the
      left and the SD at the right while the servers receive.
    - The MC stands at the deacon's right during the people's Communion.
16. **Ablutions** (Acolytes 7).
    - AC1 brings the cruets on the dish to the altar; the SD pours.
    - AC2 takes the chalice veil to the Gospel side and helps the SD build the chalice.
    - The D carries the missal across; the MC follows to the Epistle side (MC 15A).
17. **Dismissal, blessing, Last Gospel, recession.**
    - The D sings *Ite, missa est*.
    - Recession as at Sung Mass (Acolytes 8–9; Thurifer 6).

## Disagreements and parish-specific practice to label as "varies"

- **Who removes the missal at the incensations.** MC (Fortescue's "greater weight of
  authority", p. 101 n. 1), or the thurifer (Martinucci), or AC1 (FSSP Omaha, Sung Mass).
- **Who puts incense in for the elevation at a sung Mass.**
  - The crucifer at the *Hanc igitur* (FSSP Omaha).
  - The thurifer or the second acolyte at *Qui pridie* (Fortescue p. 142).
- **Who incenses at the elevation at a Solemn Mass.** The MC or the thurifer (Fortescue
  p. 104 n. 4, "most authors leave the choice open"). FSSP Omaha: the thurifer.
- **Bells at High Mass.** Fortescue: not necessary and not rung in Rome; if customary,
  only at the *Sanctus* and elevations (pp. 104–105, 142). FSSP Omaha: also at the
  *Hanc igitur* and the *Domine, non sum dignus*.
- **Servers' Confiteor before their Communion.** Said by FSSP Omaha; omitted by the 1962
  rubric (CR60 n. 503).
- **Thurifer bowing while the celebrant is incensed at the Introit of a Solemn Mass.**
  FSSP Omaha: does not. Fortescue: bows with the deacon (p. 91).
- **Acolytes at the seats** during the Gloria and Creed. They assist at the sedilia
  (Fortescue p. 95), or sit at their own places (FSSP Omaha).
